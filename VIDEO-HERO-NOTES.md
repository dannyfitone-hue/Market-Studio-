# Market Studio cinematic hero

A silent 12-second H.264 MP4 loop with six two-second industry scenes. Existing images are preserved and animated as dimensional campaign screens with perspective, camera orbit, soft shadows and short dissolves. A matching dissolve connects the final shot back to the first.

Desktop: public/marketing/assets/reel/market-studio-loop-desktop.mp4 (1920x1080, 30 fps).
Mobile: public/marketing/assets/reel/market-studio-loop-mobile.mp4 (720x1040, 30 fps).
Still posters are in the same directory.

The website automatically chooses the screen format, plays muted, loops, provides a visible pause button, pauses offscreen, and respects reduced-motion and data-saving preferences unless the visitor opts in. On browsers that block autoplay, the poster and Play reel button remain available.

Hero styling and behavior: public/marketing/hero-film.css and hero-film.js.
New-package onboarding remains unfinished from the earlier design version; this change does not modify ordering or payments.
